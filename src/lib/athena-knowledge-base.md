# PitchCoach Ai — Athena Knowledge Base

## Platform Overview
PitchCoach Ai is an AI-powered pitch coaching platform built by Athena Agentic in Abuja, Nigeria. It helps founders and entrepreneurs master their pitch using cutting-edge AI models from Cloudflare Workers AI.

## Pricing Tiers (all one-time payments, NGN default)
1. **JJC (Free):** 2 deck + 2 script sessions, basic AI feedback, community access
2. **Intern (₦9,000):** 10 deck + 10 script, detailed scores, visual audit, script rewrites
3. **Cofounder (₦15,000):** 25 deck + 25 script + 5 live + 1 full session, investor readiness scoring
4. **Founder (₦30,000):** Unlimited deck/script/live + 5 full sessions + founder coaching + investor research + cohort matching

## Coaching Modules
- **E1 Pitch Deck Analyser:** Upload PDF/PPTX, get scored on 10-slide framework, visual audit
- **E2 Script Check:** Submit script, get element-by-element feedback + AI rewrites
- **E3 Live Pitch:** Record video, get delivery + body language analysis
- **E4 Full Pitch Session:** 30-min video + deck, 6-dimension investor readiness score
- **E5 Founder Coaching:** Pathway recommendations, investor research, cohort matching, TTS narration

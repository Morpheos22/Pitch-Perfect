---
name: investor-pitch-deck-builder
description: Build and critique investor-facing pitch decks and narratives.
---

Use this skill for pitch-deck creation, review, or restructuring. Keep the narrative concise and evidence-backed: problem, customer, solution, why now, market, traction, business model, go-to-market, competition, moat, team, financial plan, raise, and use of funds. Flag unsupported claims, missing metrics, unclear ICP, weak differentiation, and inconsistent numbers. Produce an actionable slide-by-slide outline and a diligence question list. For source-heavy analysis, use the asynchronous Poke diligence capability exposed by Athena MCP when present.

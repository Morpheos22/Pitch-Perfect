Use this skill when analyzing a company, pitch deck, market, competitors, moat, traction, risks, or investment readiness. Separate verified facts, company claims, assumptions, and unknowns. Prefer primary sources, cite URLs and dates, test market-size and growth claims, inspect unit economics and retention, map competitors and defensibility, and finish with risks, open questions, and decision-relevant next steps. For large research or deck-parsing tasks, delegate to the asynchronous Poke diligence capability when an Athena MCP tool named triggerDeepDiligence or pokeDiligenceJob is available; do not invent an unavailable tool or endpoint.
